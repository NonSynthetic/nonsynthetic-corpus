==================================================

Core Product
------------
api.nonsynthetic.net: 443 priced tools ($0.002–$5/call), monetized via
x402 (USDC on Base + Solana) and Stripe checkout ($5–$100 top-ups).

Agent Task Board
----------------

on-chain. No KYC, no custody — pure pay-per-execution.
